## 4. Concept projects

### Serein Objects
Category: Lifestyle and home goods. Type: Brand direction, photography, content, ecommerce experience.
Core idea: Everyday objects photographed and presented with the calm attention normally given to collectible design.
Card copy: `A visual and digital identity for a homeware concept built around useful objects, honest materials, and quieter design.`
Demonstrate: art direction, product photography, brand messaging, responsive product pages, editorial product story.

### Goodkind Coffee
Category: Food and beverage. Type: Campaign concept, film, photography, social content.
Core idea: Show the small rituals around a good cup of coffee, not just the drink itself.
Card copy: `A campaign concept that follows the people, places, and small rituals behind a neighbourhood coffee brand.`
Demonstrate: short-form film, still photography, campaign idea, copy system, practical social rollout.

### Northstar Work
Category: SaaS and technology. Type: Launch strategy, landing page, lead-generation concept.
Core idea: Make a complex work tool easier to understand by showing one clear use case at a time.
Card copy: `A launch experience for a fictional software product, built to make its value easier to grasp and its next step easier to take.`
Demonstrate: positioning, landing-page structure, UX writing, web development, form flow, analytics plan.

### Case template sections
Cover (title, type, industry, services, label, hero visual). 01 The brief. 02 The opportunity. 03 The direction. 04 The work (labeled gallery with captions). 05 The details (two or three decisions). 06 The outcome or intended measure (proposed measures only, phrased as `If launched, success would be measured by`). 07 Next project CTA: `Have a challenge of your own? Let us look at it from six angles.`
